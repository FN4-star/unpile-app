Unpile

Sort the pile. Keep what matters.

Unpile turns your YouTube playlists into poster cards, like a streaming app. Go through them one video at a time, choose Keep or Let go, then clear the clutter in a single confirmed batch.

What it does

  • Shows your own playlists, your Liked videos and an imported Watch Later list as rows of cards.
  • Lets you mark each video Keep or Let go. Nothing changes on YouTube until you confirm in a Review list.
  • Removes videos from playlists and un-likes videos you chose to let go, using YouTube's own API.
  • Files kept videos from your inbox rows into the playlist you pick.

Self-hosted by design

Unpile runs on your own computer or home server. Your YouTube data and sign-in token are stored there and nowhere else. There is no Unpile cloud service, no account with us, and no tracking.

Unpile asks for permission to manage your YouTube account (the youtube scope) because removing videos from playlists and un-liking them requires it. Read the privacy policy for exactly how that access is used.
