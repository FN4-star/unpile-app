Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Unpile is a self-hosted app made by YOUR NAME ("I", "me"). It runs on the computer or server of the person who installs it. This policy explains what data Unpile touches and where it goes.

The short version

Your data stays on the machine that runs Unpile. I do not receive, collect, store, sell or share any of it.

What Unpile accesses

When you sign in with Google, you grant Unpile access to your YouTube account through the YouTube Data API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). Unpile uses it to:

  • read your playlists, the videos in them, and your Liked videos list;
  • read public details of those videos (title, thumbnail, duration, description);
  • remove videos from your playlists, add videos to a playlist, and un-like videos, only after you confirm in the Review list.

If you import a Google Takeout Watch Later file, Unpile reads the video IDs in it, then looks up their public details.

Where the data is stored

Unpile saves a copy of playlist and video details, your Keep and Let go choices, a job queue, and your Google sign-in token in a folder on the machine where you run it. Nothing is sent to me or to any third party. Delete that folder and the data is gone.

Sharing and transfer

Unpile sends your data only to Google and YouTube, to perform the actions above. It does not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling or training AI models, and no person reads it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Unpile's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

Unpile uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Revoke Unpile's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Unpile data folder on your machine to erase everything it stored.

Children

Unpile is not directed at children under 13.
